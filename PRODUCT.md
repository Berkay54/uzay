# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Vite + GSAP ScrollTrigger + Lenis. Hero video is shipped as a WebP frame sequence drawn to canvas so scroll scrubbing stays smooth. Static build, no backend.

## Users

Design-award juries and showcase browsers, plus curious people sent a link. Desktop first, full attention, a few minutes.

## Product Purpose

A concept, award-style scroll experience in Turkish: "How far can the naked eye see?" The hero scrubs a supplied video (camera enters an eye, passes the pupil, emerges into space). The page then travels outward through what a person can see without instruments, from the Moon to the Andromeda Galaxy (2.5 million light-years), and ends by telling the visitor how to see Andromeda tonight.

## Positioning

The eye is both the starting point and the instrument: every object shown is one the visitor can verify with their own eyes, at its true distance.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Hero asset: user's video `Camera_entering_eye_to_space_788dcaf2_1080p.mp4` (10 s, 24 fps), shipped as frames in `public/frames/`.
- Turkish copy; every distance and fact must be true.
- Real photographs (NASA/ESA public domain; Moon CC BY-SA 3.0 Gregory H. Revera; Andromeda CC BY 2.0 Adam Evans) with visible credits.
- Must work on mobile and honor reduced motion.

## Brand Commitments

User feedback from a deleted first attempt: the subject must read literally and immediately (real space, real eye). No metaphorical worlds the visitor has to decode.

## Evidence on Hand

The hero video and the seven sourced photographs in `public/img/`. No testimonials, credits, or press; none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Literal first: what the visitor sees is what the copy says.
2. Every number is true.
3. The eye is the protagonist: the page opens in an eye and every object is naked-eye visible.
4. End with an action the visitor can take tonight.
